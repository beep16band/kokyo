# Production prompts and reference provenance

Artwork was regenerated with the built-in image editor, using the supplied images as visual references. Rejected draft images were not packed. Runtime PNGs are under `kotoba-shop/assets/characters/drawn/`.

## Common production prompt for the eight residents

Production chibi anime RPG sprite atlas. Use reference lower village residents panel ONLY to preserve this one character: [CHARACTER]. Generate only this SAME character in exactly 4 columns and 4 rows, sixteen equal square cells on a square transparent PNG. Rich cute anime hand-painted shading and crisp linework, same style as reference. Row1 DOWN/front, row2 LEFT profile nose left, row3 RIGHT profile nose right, row4 UP/rear NO FACE. Every row FOUR walking phases: LEFT foot forward, neutral, RIGHT foot forward, neutral. Alternate legs and arms with an actual left-right stride. Match consistent head/body size and clothes throughout. Full-body characters, centered in each cell, identical sole baseline, generous empty space around hair and feet. No other residents, no teacher, no title, lettering, panel, border, checkerboard, background, floor, shadow or props. Entire background must be truly alpha transparent. Do NOT paint a backdrop. Exactly four visible sprites across every row.

Reference: `ちびキャラ村の住民図鑑.png`, lower residents panel.

- akari: cheerful brown bob-haired girl in warm burnt-orange floral dress with cream collar and brown shoes.
- haruka: quiet girl with long straight dark blue hair, blue dress with pale trim, brown shoes.
- sota: cheerful boy in green cap, beige shirt, GREEN vest, khaki shorts and brown shoes.
- takumi: crafting boy with curly brown hair, small ROUND GLASSES, blue denim overalls over cream shirt and brown shoes.
- fumi: kind girl with dark purple bob hair and small yellow hairclip, lavender dress and brown shoes.
- ken: active boy in BLUE cap, white shirt with BLUE trim, dark blue shorts and sneakers.
- mio: fashionable honey-blond girl, two small pigtails with pink bows, pale pink dress, brown shoes.
- masaru: gentle stocky boy, short dark brown hair, green polo shirt with cream collar, beige shorts and brown shoes.

## Main character and teacher prompts

The same 4×4 production layout, transparent background, whole-body padding, front/left/right/rear views and four walking phases were requested using these individual reference identities:

- boy — `少年主人公のちびキャラスプライトシート.png`: straw hat BLUE ribbon, brown short hair, white shirt, BLUE overalls, brown shoes, small brown satchel. The final prompt explicitly required a true rear view, no reference lettering/panels/checkerboard, stable clothes and hat size, and four separated phases per direction.
- girl — `女主人公キャラチップ素材シート.png`: straw hat pink/red ribbon, brown hair in two short pigtails, white blouse and coral red dress, brown shoes, small brown cross-body satchel.
- sakamoto — `阪本先生のちびキャラスプライトシート.png`: pretty adult female shopkeeper, dark brown ponytail, cream blouse, BROWN APRON and brown skirt, dark shoes.
- sakaguchi — `阪口先生の多彩なキャラチップ集.png`: friendly slightly stocky young male teacher, short dark brown hair, BLACK ROUND GLASSES, sage green cardigan over white shirt, brown trousers, brown shoes, small brown satchel.
- galaxy — `筋肉質な先生のキャラチップシート.png`: muscular athletic male teacher, short spiky black hair, sunglasses resting on head, broad TEETH SHOWING SMILE, WHITE sleeveless athletic tank with blue trim, navy shorts, blue sports shoes, whistle necklace.

The editor was instructed to preserve the richly drawn chibi reference aesthetic rather than substitute geometric mannequins. The resulting alpha, cell spacing, direction, sole alignment and animation were inspected and technically packed; prompting alone was not treated as proof of correct output. See `README.md` for actual animation reuse and remaining bespoke-pose scope.
